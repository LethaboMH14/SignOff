## Submission

The dashboard asks for three things, and Submit unlocks when all are present:

1. Project title
2. Public repository URL
3. Demo video URL (at most three minutes, per the participant guide)

## Track requirements

From the Ripple Agentic Finance brief, as recorded in this repository:

1. An autonomous transaction on XRPL Testnet or Devnet.
2. Limits enforced outside the agent's control.
3. A way for a human operator to set or change those limits.

## How to present

- Two thirds of the score is product and business. Open with the customer and the cost, not the ledger.
- Show one flow working live, then one attack failing live.
- Label every simulated part before a judge asks.
- Answer the South African legal question first, in one sentence, and say no lawyer has reviewed it.
- Keep a recorded backup of the demo. Testnets stall.
- Speakers at the event include people from banking, insurance and venture capital. Expect questions about who pays, who is liable and why a supplier would accept this.
