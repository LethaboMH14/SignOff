# SignOff research

This is a source index for the proposed product. Findings about customers, payment partners and production legality need direct validation.

- A South African solar distributor warns customers about communications claiming its banking details changed. This is an example of the risk, not a measurement of SignOff's target market. [Rubicon](https://www.rubicon.tech/)
- Ember estimates 117% year-on-year growth in Zambian solar installations in its 2026 analysis. [Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/the-take-off-in-african-solar-that-official-statistics-cant-yet-see/)
- Wholesale distribution is concentrated in Zambian towns including Lusaka, and South African ports are among the routes for imported goods. [International Trade Administration](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/zambia-distribution-and-sales-channels)
- The Solar Guys lists a Lusaka address and solar products. It is a possible interview prospect, with no recorded relationship or validation. [The Solar Guys](https://www.solarguyszambia.com/contact-us)
- Ripple announced institutional RLUSD partnerships with Yellow Card, Chipper Cash and VALR. Yellow Card describes ZAR bank payouts and ZMW mobile money availability. Verify whether a particular business and corridor qualify and whether RLUSD can be used for the proposed flow. [Ripple](https://ripple.com/ripple-press/ripples-usd-backed-stablecoin-rlusd-arrives-in-Africa/) · [Yellow Card](https://help.yellowcard.io/articles/8016607239-supported-fiat-receive-send-corridors-by-country)
- South Africa's cross-border crypto manual was published for comment as a draft. Check current law and obtain jurisdiction-specific advice before any real-money product. [SARB](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/media-releases/2026/crypto-assets)
- XRPL supports permission delegation and multisigning. The proposed combination must be measured on Testnet. [Delegation](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/accounts/permission-delegation) · [Multi-signing](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/accounts/multi-signing)
- The XRPL agent wallet skill, Privy, xrpl-up, MPP, x402, vaults and lending are resources for comparison and later exploration. The initial milestone is the controlled RLUSD payment. [XRPL agent wallet skill](https://xrpl.org/docs/agents/xrpl-agent-wallet-skill) · [Privy](https://docs.privy.io/recipes/agent-integrations/overview) · [xrpl-up](https://github.com/ripple/xrpl-up) · [MPP](https://mpp.dev/payment-methods/xrpl) · [x402](https://xrpl-x402.t54.ai/) · [vaults](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/single-asset-vaults) · [lending](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/lending-protocol)
